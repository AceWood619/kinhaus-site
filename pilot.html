Pilot program and beta data terms

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Kinhaus is run by Mason Wyman in Chico, California. This page explains what applying to the pilot means, what a pilot home agrees to, and how the optional beta data program works.

In short: applying is free and doesn't bind you or us. If you're selected, you'll get a written Pilot Agreement to read and sign before any device is sent. That signed agreement is what governs the pilot. The beta data program is optional, off unless you choose it, and never includes audio or video.

1. Applying

  1. Applying is free. It is not an order, a purchase, a reservation or a pre-sale, and it doesn't promise you a place in the pilot.
  2. You must be 18 or older and live in the United States.
  3. We pick pilot homes to cover a mix of home types and setups. We may not reply to every application, and we may close the pilot at any time.
  4. We use what you send only to run the pilot and contact you about it, as described in our privacy policy. You can ask us to delete your application at any time.

2. If you're selected: what the Pilot Agreement covers

The full Pilot Agreement will include at least these points. Nothing ships until both sides have signed it.

  1. Loan, not sale. Pilot devices are prototypes lent to you at no cost. Kinhaus keeps ownership, and you return them at the end of the pilot unless we agree otherwise in writing.
  2. Prototypes. Pilot devices are pre-production and may have bugs. They're provided as-is for testing, not as finished products. They are provided only in ways that comply with FCC rules for devices that are not yet authorized for sale.
  3. Safety. You'll follow the setup and safety instructions, keep devices away from water and out of reach of young children, and stop using a device and tell us if anything seems wrong.
  4. Your role. Short feedback check-ins and an optional short survey. You may quit the pilot at any time and return the devices.
  5. Feedback. You allow Kinhaus to use your feedback and suggestions to improve its products, without payment. We won't publish your name or quotes without your separate written permission.
  6. Confidentiality. We'll ask you not to share unreleased details we mark as confidential. Your own opinions and experiences are always yours to share.
  7. Privacy. Devices keep working locally, as on the main site. Data only reaches Kinhaus if you join the beta data program below.
  8. No payment either way. You don't pay for the loan, and the pilot doesn't entitle you to a price, discount or free device unless the signed agreement says so.
  9. Law. The agreement is governed by California law.

3. Beta data program (optional)

Pilot homes can choose to send us information that helps us find bugs and improve the product. It's off unless you opt in, separately from applying, and you can leave at any time. No Kinhaus device sends this data today; it will only run on pilot devices whose owners opt in.

  1. Use. Only to fix problems and improve Kinhaus products. Never for advertising, and never sold or shared for others' marketing.
  2. Who sees it. The Kinhaus team, and service providers that store it for us under contract.
  3. How long. Up to 12 months after collection, or sooner if you ask us to delete it.
  4. Leaving. Turn the setting off in Home Assistant or email us. Sending stops right away, and on request we delete what we hold.
  5. Changes. If we ever want to collect anything new, we'll ask you first. We won't add it silently.
