Home AI computer · In design

The Kinhaus Hub

The home's own brain. Home Assistant and a private AI assistant on hardware you own, so even the smart answers never leave the house.

  • AIAn NVIDIA Jetson AI computer inside, sized to run open language and voice models with no cloud
  • Model choiceNot tied to one AI company. Swap in a newer open model whenever a better one appears
  • Smart-home radiosThread and Zigbee built in, with room to add more
  • VoiceFar-field microphones, a speaker and a hardware mute that cuts the mics
  • CameraOptional video calls on your TV, with a physical shutter and power cut-off for privacy
  • SensingAir quality, climate, light and presence for the room it sits in

The Hub is in circuit design and not for sale. We will publish measured power use before launch.

Why a hub

Smart answers that never leave the house

No cloud AI required

Questions, routines and voice commands are understood on the Hub itself. Nothing is sent to an AI company to work.

Not tied to one model

AI models improve every few months. The Hub is built to run whichever open model is best, so it gets better without a new subscription.

Honest about power

A home AI computer uses electricity. We'll publish the Hub's measured idle and peak power before launch.
