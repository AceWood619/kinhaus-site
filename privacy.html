Privacy and waitlist terms

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Kinhaus is run by Mason Wyman in Chico, California. This page explains what we collect on kinhaushome.com, how we use it, what joining the waitlist means, and what Kinhaus devices send.

What we collect

If you join the waitlist: your email address and, only if you choose to answer, which devices interest you and whether you run Home Assistant.

If you apply to be a pilot home: your name, email, city and state, the kind of home you have, how many rooms you'd cover, whether you run Home Assistant, anything you write in the application, and whether you opted in to the beta data program.

If you email us, we have whatever you put in that email.

The website sets no cookies and uses no advertising trackers or analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log basic technical data (such as IP address and browser type) to keep the site running. The page loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google's servers when it opens the site.

Why we use it

To send you the Kinhaus updates you asked for, to run the pilot program and contact applicants, and to understand which devices people want. That's all.

What we don't do

We don't sell or share your personal information, and we don't use it for targeted advertising.

Who handles it for us

Our email provider stores waitlist sign-ups, applications and messages and sends updates, and GitHub hosts the website. They may use your data only to provide that service to us. If you send a tip through a tip-jar service, that service handles the payment; we never see your card details.

How long we keep it

Waitlist details: until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them, or until we stop sending updates. Pilot applications: until the pilot ends, then deleted unless you're a pilot home or ask us to keep you in mind. Beta data: up to 12 months, as described in the beta data terms.

Your choices

Every update email has an unsubscribe link. You can also ask us what we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it, by writing to hello@kinhaushome.com. We won't treat you differently for asking. California residents may have additional rights under California privacy law where it applies.

Children

The waitlist and the pilot are for adults. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 18.

Our devices

Kinhaus devices work without a Kinhaus account or cloud. What a device senses stays on your own network and is governed by your own Home Assistant setup.

Update checks. Devices check our public firmware page (hosted on GitHub) about once an hour for new versions and download updates from it. The request contains no personal information or sensor data, but like any web request it shows your home's IP address to GitHub. Automatic installs are on by default and can be switched off in Home Assistant.

Beta data program. Pilot homes can choose to send device health and usage statistics to Kinhaus. It is off unless you opt in, never includes audio or video, and you can leave at any time. Details are in the beta data terms.

Waitlist terms

  1. Joining the waitlist is free. It is not an order, a reservation or a pre-sale, and no product, price or ship date is promised.
  2. We'll email you updates about Kinhaus. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  3. If we ever offer pre-orders, they'll come with their own terms and you'll choose whether to take part.
  4. We may close or change the waitlist at any time.

Pilot program

Applying to the pilot doesn't bind you or us. Selected homes sign a separate Pilot Agreement before any device is sent. See the pilot program and beta data terms.

Tips

Kinhaus is a for-profit company in development, not a charity. A tip is a gift to help build it. It isn't tax-deductible, and it doesn't buy a device, a reservation or a discount.

Changes

If this page changes, we'll post the new version here with a new date. We won't start collecting a new kind of data from devices without asking first.
