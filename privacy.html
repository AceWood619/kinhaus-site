Privacy and waitlist terms

Last updated: November 2026

Kinhaus is run by Mason Wyman in Chico, California. This page explains what we collect on kinhaushome.com, how we use it, and what joining the waitlist means.

What we collect

If you join the waitlist: your email address and, only if you choose to answer, which devices interest you and whether you run Home Assistant. If you email us, we have whatever you put in that email.

We don't use advertising trackers or analytics. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log basic technical data (such as IP address and browser type) to keep the site running. The page loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google's servers when it opens the site.

Why we use it

To send you the Kinhaus updates you asked for, and to understand which devices people want. That's all.

What we don't do

We don't sell or share your personal information, and we don't use it for targeted advertising.

Who handles it for us

Our email-list provider stores the waitlist and sends updates, and GitHub hosts the website. They may use your data only to provide that service to us. If you send a tip through a tip-jar service, that service handles the payment; we never see your card details.

How long we keep it

Until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it, or until we stop sending updates.

Your choices

Every update email has an unsubscribe link. You can also ask us what we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it, by writing to hello@kinhaushome.com. We won't treat you differently for asking. California residents may have additional rights under California privacy law where it applies.

Children

The waitlist is for adults. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 18.

Our devices

Kinhaus devices are designed to work without a Kinhaus account or cloud. This page covers the website and the waitlist. What a device senses stays on your own network and is governed by your own Home Assistant setup.

Waitlist terms

  1. Joining the waitlist is free. It is not an order, a reservation or a pre-sale, and no product, price or ship date is promised.
  2. We'll email you updates about Kinhaus. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  3. If we ever offer pre-orders, they'll come with their own terms and you'll choose whether to take part.
  4. We may close or change the waitlist at any time.

Tips

Kinhaus is a for-profit company in development, not a charity. A tip is a gift to help build it. It isn't tax-deductible, and it doesn't buy a device, a reservation or a discount.

Changes

If this page changes, we'll post the new version here with a new date.
