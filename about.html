About

Built in one home first

Kinhaus is a small company in Chico, California building smart-home devices that answer to the people who live with them.

From the founder

Built in my own home first

"I wanted a smart home that stays smart when the internet goes out, and doesn't ship my family's life to someone else's servers. Nobody made it, so I started building it. Every Kinhaus device lives on my own walls before it goes on yours."

Mason Wyman · Founder, Kinhaus · Chico, California

Building in the open

Where we are

Real progress instead of a launch date. Nothing here is for sale yet, and there is no price.

  1. Oct 2026

    Hardware designed

    Prototype circuit boards and the Home Assistant integration designed.

  2. Nov 2026

    The stealth saucer

    The enclosure redesigned from scratch, printed and refined on the bench.

  3. Nov 2026

    Living in a real home

    Released firmware for every Node model. Three Nodes run the founder's home every day.

  4. Nov 2026

    Hub circuit design

    The Hub's power, AI module, networking, audio, radio and sensor circuits are drawn and checked.

  5. Now

    First prototype boards

    A small first batch of five prototype boards arrived in November 2026. Each one is being flashed and bench-tested now.

  6. Next

    Ten-unit pilot batch

    A small validated batch, then a pilot in 5 to 15 real homes.

  7. After the pilot

    Decide what comes next

    What the pilot homes teach us decides the next step. Waitlist members hear about it first.
