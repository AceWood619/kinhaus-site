Setup

On the wall in minutes

No app to download and no account to make. If you run Home Assistant, you already have what you need.

  1. Hang it

    Two screws, a keyhole mount and a USB-C cable. No electrician, renter-friendly.

  2. Adopt it

    Home Assistant finds the Node on your network and gives it its own key. Tell it which room it's in.

  3. Live with it

    Lights, climate and voice start following who is actually in each room.
